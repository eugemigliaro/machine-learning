The TP3 guidelines are now availab le in the TPs folder, and the dataset can be found on Campus in the Competición folder.
We explained the TP3 Machine Learning competition in class the other day, so you can check the recording for more details. In any case, here is a summary of the most important information:
Deliverables
As usual, all deliverables must be submitted the day before each group's TP3 presentation.
Each team must submit:
- Predictor function, which will be used to evaluate the model in the competition. It must run independently, take an input feature vector (X) with the expected dataset dimensions, and return the predicted output (y).
- Notebook and presentation (PPT), following the usual TP format.
Additional predictor submission dates (for the competition)
There will be three submission opportunities, with the leaderboard updated after each deadline:
- November 4, 11 and 18, always before 4:00 PM.
Each team may submit only once (on the date corresponding to their TP3 presentation) or on all three dates if they wish. The latest submission will be used for the competition.
Final Score
Competition Score = 90% × Model Score (November 18 submission) + 10% × Presentation Grade
Prizes
The following teams will be exempt from the December final exam:
- TOP 1 – Model Score: The team with the highest predictive performance.
- TOP 4 – Competition Score: The four highest-ranked teams based on the combined score.
As a condition, the winning teams must give a short final presentation on November 25, our last class, including both a technical and a non-technical explanation of their solution.
If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
Good luck with the competition!
